#### Spawnable Weapons
In the future, I would like to see little challenge areas, loot locations, or more monsters, which would provide a source for more weapons and such. Currently, the following firearms are obtainable:

##### Easier Drops
-M16a4 (secubot/TALON UGV disassembly)
-M9 (feral security guard, zombie security guard drop)

##### Random drops from zombies
-"guns_pistol_common_everyday_carry" inside "default_zombie_death_drops"
-"bugout_bag" inside "default_zombie_death_drops"

NOTE: As of writing, the bugout drops (in other words, random drops from zombies at a low chance) include ALL non-milspec shotguns, including the likes of the Saiga 12. This could be unbalanced.

#### Materials
Material discs are meant to be easy enough to carry, but for the more powerful materials to be heavier and less convenient than the others.

Material costs for items will likely need fine tuning.

Quality Material Discs are not just for unique items, and are meant to be used in recipes for powerful firearms, equipment, and weapon mods.
